## 🛠️ PHASE 1: THE FOUNDATIONS (Structural Mastery)
*Levels A1 to A2+ - Deconstructing the core mechanics of everyday communication.*

### **Year 1 (Level A1 - Structural Basics)**
**Goal**: Master the mechanics of present-tense syntax, noun-adjective relationships, and foundational sentence building.

1. **Unit 1: Identity and Spatial Relationships (A1)**
    - 1.1 The Copula: Verb "To Be" and Subject Pronouns 
    - 1.2 Definite/Indefinite Articles and Pluralization Rules 
    - 1.3 Demonstrative Determiners and Spatial Deixis 
    - 1.4 Possessive Determiners vs. Pronouns 
    - 1.5 Existential "There" (There is / There are) 
    - 1.6 Prepositions of Place and Spatial Awareness 
    - 1.7 Attributive and Predicative Adjectives 
    - 1.8 Descriptive and Proper Adjectives 
    - 1.9 Numeral Adjectives and Quantifiers 

2. **Unit 2: Routine and Habitual Actions (A1)**
    - 2.1 The Simple Present: Affirmative Syntax 
    - 2.2 The Simple Present: Negation Strategies 
    - 2.3 The Simple Present: Interrogative Formations 
    - 2.4 Information Questions (Wh- Diagnostics) 
    - 2.5 Object Pronouns and Transitivity 
    - 2.6 Adverbs of Frequency and Syntax Placement 
    - 2.7 Prepositions of Time (In, On, At rules) 

3. **Unit 3: Progressive Actions and Quantities (A1-A2)**
    - 3.1 The Present Progressive: Dynamic Actions 
    - 3.2 Stative vs. Dynamic Verbs (Nuances) 
    - 3.3 Indefinite Adjectives and Pronouns 
    - 3.4 Advanced Quantifiers (Much, Many, A lot of) 
    - 3.5 Countable vs. Uncountable Noun Traps (Advice, Furniture, News) 
    - 3.6 Distributive Quantifiers (Each, Every, Both, Neither) 
    - 3.7 Core Modals: Can / Can't (Ability vs. Permission) 
    - 3.8 The Imperative Mood: Commands and Nuanced Instructions 

### **Year 2 (Level A2 - Pre-Intermediate Consolidation)**
**Goal**: Narrative sequencing, comparative mechanics, and intentional future framing.

4. **Unit 4: Temporal Narration and The Past (A2)**
    - 4.1 The Copula in the Past (Was/Were) 
    - 4.2 Existential "There" in the Past Context 
    - 4.3 Morphological Rules of Regular Past Verbs 
    - 4.4 High-Frequency Irregular Verbs in the Past Simple 
    - 4.5 Past Simple Narrative Sequencing 
    - 4.6 The Past Continuous: Background Actions 
    - 4.7 Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS in practice) 

5. **Unit 5: Comparative Scales and Future Intentions (A2)**
    - 5.1 Equivalence: The "As... As" Structure 
    - 5.2 Comparative and Superlative Morphology 
    - 5.3 Future Intentions: "Going To" vs. Present Continuous 
    - 5.4 The Simple Future with "Will" (Spontaneous vs. Planned) 

6. **Unit 6: Advanced Pronouns and Modal Expansion (A2)**
    - 6.1 Reflexive and Intensive Pronouns 
    - 6.2 Modal Verbs of Request and Polite Permission (Can, Could, May, Would) 
    - 6.3 Modals of Obligation (Must vs. Have To differences) 
    - 6.4 Modals of Advice (Should, Ought to, Had better) 
    - 6.5 Modals of Deduction and Probability (May, Might, Must be) 
    - 6.6 Prepositions of Movement and Direction 
    - 6.7 Collocations: Adjective + Preposition (Interested in, Proud of) 
    - 6.8 Collocations: Verb + Preposition Combinations 

### **Year 3 (Level A2+ to B1 - The Threshold)**
**Goal**: Bridging past experiences to present realities and hypothetical logic.

7. **Unit 7: Experiential Timelines (B1)**
    - 7.1 Nuances of "Been" vs. "Gone" 
    - 7.2 Temporal Adverbs (Just, Already, Yet, Still) 
    - 7.3 Duration Markers (For vs. Since) 
    - 7.4 Tense Contrast: Present Perfect vs. Past Simple 
    - 7.5 Present Perfect: Unspecified Past vs. Results in the Present 

8. **Unit 8: Complex Intermediate Structures (B1)**
    - 8.1 The First Conditional: Real Possibilities 
    - 8.2 "Used to" for Discontinued Past Habits 
    - 8.3 Introduction to the Passive Voice (Present & Past Simple) 
    - 8.4 Basic Relative Clauses (Who, Which, That restrictions) 
    - 8.5 Degree Mechanisms: "Too" and "Enough" 
    - 8.6 Phrasal Verbs: Separable vs. Inseparable Mechanics

## 🚀 PHASE 2: ADVANCED FLUENCY (Pre-University Level)
*Level B1+ to B2 - Complexity, Abstraction, and Fluency.*

9. **Unit 9: Perfect Tenses and Complex Futures (B1+)**
    - 9.1 Present Perfect Continuous: Focus on Duration 
    - 9.2 The Past Perfect (Had done): Chronology of the Past 
    - 9.3 The Future Continuous (Will be doing) 
    - 9.4 The Future Perfect (Will have done) and Future Viewpoints 

10. **Unit 10: Hypothesis and Irreality (B2)**
    - 10.1 The Second Conditional: Unreal Present/Future 
    - 10.2 The Third Conditional: Regrets and Past Alternatives 
    - 10.3 Mixed Conditionals: Blending Timelines 
    - 10.4 The Subjunctive Mood in Wishes (I wish / If only) 

11. **Unit 11: Passive Nuances and Reported Speech (B2)**
    - 11.1 The Passive Voice Across All Tenses 
    - 11.2 The Causative Form (Have/Get something done) 
    - 11.3 Reported Speech Mechanisms: Tense Backshifting 
    - 11.4 Advanced Reporting Verbs (Suggest, Advise, Accuse) 

12. **Unit 12: Cohesion, Style, and Non-Finite Clauses (B2+)**
    - 12.1 Gerunds vs. Infinitives: Semantic Shifts (Stop, Remember, Try) 
    - 12.2 Complex Verb Patterns (Verb + Object + Infinitive) 
    - 12.3 Modals of Past Deduction (Must have, Might have, Can't have) 
    - 12.4 Relative Clauses: Defining vs. Non-Defining (Restrictive vs. Non-restrictive) 
    - 12.5 Conjunctive Adverbs for Contrast and Addition (However, Moreover, Furthermore)

## 🏛️ PHASE 3: LINGUISTIC MASTERY (University / Native-Like Proficiency)
*Level C1 to C2 - Academic English, Rhetoric, and Pragmatics.*

13. **Unit 13: Sophisticated Syntactic Structures (C1)**
    - 13.1 The Future Perfect Continuous 
    - 13.2 The Mandative Subjunctive in Formal English (I suggest he go) 
    - 13.3 Participle Clauses (-ing and -ed clauses for sentence reduction) 
    - 13.4 Nominal Relative Clauses (Whoever, Whatever) 

14. **Unit 14: Emphasis, Rhetoric, and Stylistics (C1-C2)**
    - 14.1 Cleft Sentences (It was X that... / What I need is...) 
    - 14.2 Fronting and Negative Inversion (Never have I..., Seldom do we...) 
    - 14.3 Nominalization (Turning verbs/adjectives into noun phrases for formal writing) 
    - 14.4 Advanced Textual Cohesion: Anaphoric and Cataphoric References 
    - 14.5 Punctuation for Rhetorical Impact (The em-dash, semicolon, and colon usage) 

15. **Unit 15: Specialized English and Pragmatics (C2)**
    - 15.1 Advanced Idioms and Academic Collocations 
    - 15.2 Deep Register Nuances: Ultra-Formal vs. Slang/Colloquialisms 
    - 15.3 Domain-Specific Jargon: Legal, Medical, and Journalistic English 
    - 15.4 Sociolinguistics: Global Variants of English (US, UK, AUS formatting and slang)

## 📊 Evaluation Roadmap for FeliNode (Hard Mode)

### **MINIMUM VIABLE MASTERY (Solid Foundation)**
✅ **Structural Basics**: Flawless mastery of Present/Past/Future simple, correct pronoun usage, and core prepositions.
✅ **Narrative Control**: Confident use of perfect tenses, modals, and comparatives.
✅ **Intermediate Nuance**: Solid grasp of 1st Conditionals, Phrasal Verbs, and basic Passives.

### **PREMIUM STANDARD (Fluency Achieved)**
✅ **Advanced Fluidity:** Automatic correct usage of Gerunds vs. Infinitives.
✅ **Hypothetical Mastery:** Seamless integration of all Conditionals (0, 1, 2, 3, and Mixed).
✅ **Reported & Passive:** Deep understanding of the Causative and backshifting.

### **EXCELLENT (True Hard Mode / Differentiation)**
✅ **C1-C2 Complexity**: Inversions, Cleft sentences, and Subjunctive mood effectively used.
✅ **Certification Ready**: Prepared for advanced components of Proficiency exams (C2 Proficiency, IELTS 8.0+).
✅ **Variant Awareness**: Understanding the sociolinguistic differences across global English dialects.
